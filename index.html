Island — a voxel world you can write on

Sculpt

  • Click / drag — apply the current tool; a drag acts once per block the cursor moves into, so holding still adds nothing more
  • B build · D dig · P paint · N note · M place a model · H pan
  • M opens the model gallery: pick a prefab (tree, house, duck…), then click the world — it claims its own blocks, resting on the surface. Models are drawn at their own finer resolution, so the duck really is smaller than the house. Dig a model to take it away
  • 1…9 — pick a material
  • [ / ] — brush size (1×1, 3×3, 5×5)
  • Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Shift+Z — undo / redo (one stroke at a time)

Move around

  • Space+drag, middle-drag or right-drag — pan the infinite world
  • Wheel — zoom at the cursor · Shift+wheel — pan · pinch on touch
  • + / − / 0 — zoom in / out / recentre
  • spin / tilt sliders — orbit the camera: any angle around, and up to straight down, where note writing reads head-on
  • V — jump between the isometric look and straight down

Notes

  • Note tool or Shift+click — stick a note flat on the surface
  • Click the text — edit it in place, WYSIWYG
  • Drag a white corner handle — resize the tile; it snaps to whole blocks
  • ⠿ drags the note onto other blocks · ⤢ or double-click opens the big editor
  • ● cycles paper colour · × deletes (undo brings it back)
  • Notes rest on the highest block under them, so they follow terrain you build.

Everything is stored in this browser and saved as you work. Use ⤓ to export a JSON copy.
